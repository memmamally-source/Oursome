## What Oursome does
Oursome helps you organise your calendar, tasks, reminders, shopping lists and shared household plans.

## Information we collect
- **Account information:** name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash by our authentication provider), time zone and preferences.
- **Content you create:** events, tasks, reminders, lists, notes, announcements and the household (“Our Space”) you create or join.
- **Imports you choose to process:** text, screenshots, photos or documents you submit for extraction.
- **Subscription status:** whether you have Premium (via Apple/Google and our subscription provider [RevenueCat]). We do not receive your full payment card details.
- **Device and diagnostics:** app version, device type, crash reports and privacy-conscious usage events (for example, “task created”). Usage events do not contain the text of your tasks, events or lists.

_Current preview build:_ all data is stored only on your device and nothing is sent to our servers.

## How we use information
To provide and sync the service; to send reminders and notifications you ask for; to process imports and assistant requests you initiate; to manage subscriptions; to keep the service secure; to understand aggregate usage and improve the product; to comply with law.

We do **not** sell personal information, and we do **not** use the content of your calendar, tasks or household for advertising.
