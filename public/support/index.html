Help & support

Oursome is in preview. Thank you for trying it.

How do I add Oursome to my home screen?

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Where is my data saved?

On this device only, in this browser. If you use a different browser or phone, you'll start fresh. Cloud sync is coming in a later update.

Can I share with my partner or family?

You can create an Our Space and mark items as Shared today. Inviting other people needs cloud accounts, which are on the way.

Why didn't I get a reminder notification?

Notifications aren't switched on in the preview yet. Your reminder settings are saved and will be used once they are.

How do I remove the sample content?

Tap Clear it on the Home banner, or go to Settings → Sample content.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data, or Settings → Account → Delete account and data. See the privacy page.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit, Oursome opens without a connection.
