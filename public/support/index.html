Help & support

Questions, problems or ideas: we'd love to hear from you.

How do I make a photo album?

Tap Album on Home (or Our Space → Albums → New album), choose your photos, give it a name and tap Create album. Oursome lays out the pages for you. Tap Edit to change layouts, captions, the cover or the order of pages.

Where is my data saved?

On your device: in the app, or in the browser you use for the website. If you switch phone or browser you'll start fresh, so export your data first (Settings → Privacy & data).

Are my photos private?

Yes. They stay on your device, albums are private unless you choose to share them with your household, and photos are never used to train AI. See the privacy policy.

Can I share with my partner or family?

You can set up your household in Our Space and mark things as Shared. Syncing between different people's phones isn't switched on yet.

How do I add the website to my home screen?

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

How do I print an album?

On the website, open the album and tap PDF. Choose the print version (8 × 10 in, 300 dpi with bleed) to take to a print shop, or the smaller one for sharing.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data, or Settings → Account → Delete account and data, which erases everything on that device, photos included.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app works without a connection, and the website does too after your first visit.
