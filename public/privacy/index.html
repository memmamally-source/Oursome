Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026 · applies to the Oursome website and app

The short version: your plans, lists and photos stay on your phone. Only what you choose to share with your family, plus the account you need for that, is stored online so your family can see it. No ads, no tracking, no selling your data, and your photos are never used to train AI.

What stays on your device

Everything you mark Private, and all your photos (albums, journal photos and your household cover), are saved only on your device: in the app's own storage on a phone, or in your browser's storage on the website. Oursome only sees the photos you pick; it never browses your photo library.

What is stored online, and why

To share with your family, Oursome uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), run by Google as our data processor. It stores:

  • Your account: your name, email address and an account ID. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; Oursome never sees or stores it.
  • What you share with a family: items marked Shared in a family you've chosen to share (tasks, events, shopping lists, journal words and noticeboard notes), the family's name and its members' names and colours.
  • Invite codes you create, which expire after 7 days.

Only members of a family can read its shared items; this is enforced by the database's security rules, not just the app. Photos are not uploaded: a shared journal page shows its words to your family and its photo only on the device where it was added. If you never share a family, nothing but your account is stored online.

What Oursome doesn't do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of your information, other than with Google as described above to run family sharing.
  • Your photos and content are never used to train AI models.
  • Nothing is public. Shared means shared with the members of your family on Oursome, not with the internet.
  • The organiser and Smart Import understand what you type on your device; your text isn't sent anywhere.

Leaving a family and deleting your account

  • Stop sharing here (Our Space) takes you out of a shared family. Your copy stays on your device.
  • Settings → Account → Delete account and data deletes your online account, takes you out of every shared family and erases everything on that device, photos included. Items you shared stay with the family you shared them with, as part of that family's records; the family's admin can remove them.

Exports and printing

Album PDFs and data exports are made on your device and saved where you choose. Oursome doesn't send them anywhere.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data saves your information as a file.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account and data, as described above. You can also email us to ask for your online data to be deleted.

Children

Oursome is for adults organising family life. You can add your children's activities and photos to your own account, and they stay private on your device. Please don't create accounts for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app about anything important.
