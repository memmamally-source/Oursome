Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026 · applies to the current Oursome preview

The short version: in this preview, everything you add stays in this browser or on this phone. Oursome has no server yet, so nothing you type is sent to us or anyone else.

What Oursome stores

Your name, email address, colour and time zone; the events, tasks, reminders, shopping lists, notes and Our Space content you create; and your settings. All of it is saved in your browser's local storage (or the app's storage on a phone).

Your password is checked for strength when you create an account but is not stored.

What Oursome doesn't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • Pasted text in Smart Import and messages to the assistant are processed on your device and aren't saved unless you add the suggestions.

Our Space and sharing

Items marked Private are only for you. Items marked Shared are meant for the people in your Our Space. In this preview, sharing works only on one device; real shared spaces between accounts come later.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data downloads everything as a file.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account and data erases everything from this device. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

Children

Oursome is for adults. You can add family activities to your own calendar, but please don't create accounts for children.

Changes ahead

Later versions will add cloud accounts and sync, reminders, an AI assistant and subscriptions. Before any of that launches, this page will explain exactly what is sent where, and you'll be told in the app.
