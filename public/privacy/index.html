Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026 · applies to the Oursome website and app

The short version: everything you add to Oursome, including your photos, stays on your phone or in your browser. We don't run a server that receives it, we don't track you, we don't show ads, and your photos are never used to train AI.

What Oursome stores, and where

Your name, email address, colour and time zone; the events, tasks, reminders, shopping lists, journal pages, photo albums and household details you create; and your settings. All of it is saved on your device: in the app's own storage on a phone, or in your browser's storage on the website.

Photos you choose for albums, the journal or your household cover are copied into that same on-device storage (with smaller versions for quick display). Oursome only sees the photos you pick; it never browses your photo library.

Your password is checked for strength when you create an account but is not stored.

What Oursome doesn't do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • Your photos and content are never used to train AI models.
  • Nothing is public. Albums and other items are private unless you mark them shared, and shared means shared with your household on Oursome, not with the internet.
  • The organiser and Smart Import understand what you type on your device; your text isn't sent anywhere.

Sharing with your family

Items marked Private are only for you; items marked Shared are for the people in your household. Today, sharing works on one device. If we switch on cloud accounts and family sync in future, this page will be updated first to explain exactly what is sent, where it's stored and who can see it, and you'll be told in the app before anything leaves your device.

Exports and printing

Album PDFs and data exports are made on your device and saved where you choose. Oursome doesn't send them anywhere.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data saves your information as a file.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account and data permanently erases your account and everything Oursome stored on that device, photos included. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) does the same.

Children

Oursome is for adults organising family life. You can add your children's activities and photos to your own account, and they stay private on your device. Please don't create accounts for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app about anything important.

Contact
