We are bringing our apps together under one name: **Karoo Fox**.

Until now our apps have been published under different developer names. Karoo Fox gives them a single home, with consistent standards for design, privacy and support.

## What this means for you

- **Nothing changes in your apps.** Your accounts, data and settings stay as they are.
- **One place for help.** The [Support Centre](../../support/) links to support, privacy policies and data-deletion instructions for every app.
- **Store listings will follow.** Over the coming months, our App Store and Google Play listings will move to the Karoo Fox name. Until then, some listings may show a previous developer name.

Thank you for using our apps. We have more on the way.
